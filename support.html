Restore Purchases

Open the VIP tab and tap Restore Purchases. If you signed in with your Apple ID or Google Account, your VIP status and credits are restored on any device signed in with the same account.

Manage or Cancel a Subscription

iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Predixa AI. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Delete Your Account and Data

In the app: VIP tab → Account & Data → Delete my account and data. Deletion takes effect immediately. You can also request it by email.
